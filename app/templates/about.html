OUR INSTITUTION

Kwara State Polytechnic, Ilorin

Kwara State Polytechnic is a tertiary institution in Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria, offering technical, vocational and professional education. Its computing students apply classroom knowledge to projects that respond to real academic and community needs.

OUR STUDENT COMMUNITY

About NACOS

The Nigeria Association of Computing Students (NACOS) is a community for students studying computing-related disciplines. It supports knowledge sharing, innovation, mentorship, teamwork and professional growth.

Within Kwara State Polytechnic, NACOS students contribute to a culture of learning and collaborative problem-solving.

PROJECT SPOTLIGHT · 2026/2027

HND II — Group C

This portal is a software development project by HND II Group C students for the 2026/2027 academic session. It demonstrates practical web application design, authentication, course registration, announcements and the publication of academic calendar events.

The project is an educational initiative and should not be confused with the official institutional student portal or an official academic calendar unless content has been formally approved by the institution.
